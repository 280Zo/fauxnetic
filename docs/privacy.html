Fauxnetic privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Fauxnetic is a free app that shows how easily a voice can be cloned, so people learn to be careful with voice calls and messages. It works entirely on your phone.

What Fauxnetic collects

Nothing. Fauxnetic has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

What stays on your phone

Your recordings, the voices built from them, their transcripts and the lines you generate are stored only on your phone, encrypted with a key kept in Android's secure storage. They are deleted automatically after 24 hours (or 1 hour, if you choose that in Settings), and you can delete them at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Internet use

The app uses the internet only to download its voice models from Hugging Face, once, each file checked against a fixed fingerprint. Hugging Face receives your phone's network address as part of that download, as with any download; see Hugging Face's privacy policy. No recording, voice or line is ever uploaded.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record the voice you clone, only while you are recording.
  • Internet: to download the voice models.
  • Notifications, foreground service and wake lock: so downloads and lines keep going if you leave the app or the screen turns off, with a notification while they run.

Sharing and reporting

A line leaves your phone only if you share it yourself through Android's share sheet. Every line carries an inaudible watermark that detection tools can find. If you report a line, your email app opens a draft with the line's text and model name; nothing is sent until you send it, and no audio is attached.

Children

Fauxnetic is not directed at children.
